Our story

From one small store to your doorstep

GreenBasket opened its first 800 sq ft store in Koramangala in 2014 with a simple idea: sell only what we would happily take home to our own families. Our customers loved the fresh produce and honest prices – and soon asked if we could deliver.

Today we serve more than 50,000 families across 12 cities, working directly with 300+ farmers and local producers. We have grown, but every order is still picked by hand and checked before it leaves our store.

Our mission

Make fresh, healthy groceries easy and affordable for every household.

Our vision

A food system with zero waste, fair pay for farmers and happy kitchens.

Freshness & quality commitment

Our 5-point freshness check

Every fruit, vegetable, dairy and meat product passes the same checks before it is packed for you.

  • Sourced daily – produce arrives every morning from partner farms.
  • Cold chain – dairy, meat and seafood stay between 0–4 °C.
  • Hand-picked – our pickers choose each item as if for their own family.
  • Shelf-life rule – packaged goods have at least 70% shelf life left.
  • Doorstep guarantee – reject anything that isn’t fresh at delivery.
